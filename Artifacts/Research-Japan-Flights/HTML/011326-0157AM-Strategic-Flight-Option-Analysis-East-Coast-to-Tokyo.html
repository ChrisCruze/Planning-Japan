Price Band Analysis

Non-stop flights command a clear premium ($2,000+). 1-stop options provide the "sweet spot" for value ($1,400–$1,700). 2-stop flights drop below $1,000 but double travel time to 28+ hours.

Airport Selection

Haneda (HND) is the preferred arrival airport due to proximity to Tokyo (20 min vs 60 min). Most top-ranked flights in this search cycle successfully target HND.

Multi-City Strategy

Open-jaw tickets (e.g., into Tokyo, out of Osaka) add roughly $100–$400 to the base fare. This is likely cost-efficient when factoring in the saved Shinkansen fare (~$100) and travel time.
