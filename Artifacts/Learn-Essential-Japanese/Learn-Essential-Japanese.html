Learn Essential Japanese

Cultural Reference Guide

Mastering a core set of Japanese phrases enables traveler autonomy and deeper cultural engagement

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Greeting & Politeness
Konnichiwa
Hello / Good afternoon
kon-nee-chee-wah
Arigatō gozaimasu
Thank you very much
ah-ree-gah-toe go-zai-mass
Sumimasen
Excuse me / Sorry
soo-mee-mah-sen
Onegaishimasu
Please / I humbly request
oh-neh-gai-she-mass
🤔 Clarification
Wakarimasen
I don’t understand
wah-kah-ree-mah-sen
Yukkuri onegaishimasu
Please speak slowly
yoo-koo-ree oh-neh-gai-she-mass
🍽️ Dining
Kore o onegaishimasu
This one, please (pointing)
ko-reh oh oh-neh-gai-she-mass
Mizu o kudasai
Water, please
mee-zoo oh koo-dah-sai
Daijōbu desu ka?
Is this okay?
dye-joe-boo dess kah
Gochisōsama deshita
Thank you for the meal (after)
go-chee-so-sah-mah desh-tah
🛍️ Shopping
Ikura desu ka?
How much is this?
ee-koo-rah dess kah
Kore o kudasai
I'll take this
ko-reh oh koo-dah-sai
Kādo wa daijōbu desu ka?
Credit card OK?
kah-doh wah dye-joe-boo dess kah
🚆 Transportation
Eki wa doko desu ka?
Where is the station?
eh-kee wah doh-koh dess kah
Toire wa doko desu ka?
Where is the bathroom?
toy-reh wah doh-koh dess kah
⭐ Critical 6 Phrases
  • Konnichiwa (Hello)
  • Arigatō gozaimasu (Thank you)
  • Sumimasen (Excuse me/Sorry)
  • Onegaishimasu (Please)
  • Kore o onegaishimasu (This one)
  • Toire wa doko desu ka? (Bathroom?)
📌 Etiquette Pro Tips
• Speak slowly, not loudly
• Bow slightly when thanking
• Effort matters more than correctness
• Patience and kindness are key
