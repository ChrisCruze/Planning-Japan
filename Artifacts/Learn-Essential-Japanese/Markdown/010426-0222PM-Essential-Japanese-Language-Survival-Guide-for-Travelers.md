# Essential Japanese Language Survival Guide for Travelers

## Summary

A comprehensive cheat sheet of essential Japanese phrases for greetings, dining, transport, and emergencies, including pronunciation guides.

## Description

A one-page reference guide designed to facilitate basic communication and cultural etiquette for non-Japanese speakers in Japan.

## Insight

Mastering just six core phrases, including 'Sumimasen' and 'Arigatō gozaimasu,' is sufficient to navigate the majority of travel interactions in Japan.

## Input


# 🇯🇵 Japan Survival Japanese — One-Page Cheat Sheet

*(Pronunciation is approximate; speak slowly and confidently)*

---

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Greeting & Politeness (Use Everywhere)

**Hello / Good afternoon**
**Konnichiwa**
*kon-nee-chee-wah*

**Thank you very much**
**Arigatō gozaimasu**
*ah-ree-gah-toe go-zai-mass*

**Excuse me / Sorry / Thank you for the trouble**
**Sumimasen**
*soo-mee-mah-sen*

**Please / I humbly request**
**Onegaishimasu**
*oh-neh-gai-she-mass*

---

## 🍽️ Entering & Eating at Restaurants

**This one, please (pointing)**
**Kore o onegaishimasu**
*ko-reh oh oh-neh-gai-she-mass*

**Water, please**
**Mizu o kudasai**
*mee-zoo oh koo-dah-sai*

**Beer, please**
**Bīru o kudasai**
*bee-roo oh koo-dah-sai*

**Is this okay? / Is this fine?**
**Daijōbu desu ka?**
*dye-joe-boo dess kah*

**Thank you for the meal (after eating)**
**Gochisōsama deshita**
*go-chee-so-sah-mah desh-tah*

---

## 🚆 Getting Around & Transportation

**Where is the station?**
**Eki wa doko desu ka?**
*eh-kee wah doh-koh dess kah*

**Where is the train?**
**Densha wa doko desu ka?**
*den-shah wah doh-koh dess kah*

**Where is the bathroom?**
**Toire wa doko desu ka?**
*toy-reh wah doh-koh dess kah*

**Which line?**
**Nan-sen desu ka?**
*nahn-sen dess kah*

---

## 🛍️ Shopping & Paying

**How much is this?**
**Ikura desu ka?**
*ee-koo-rah dess kah*

**I’ll take this**
**Kore o kudasai**
*ko-reh oh koo-dah-sai*

**Credit card OK?**
**Kādo wa daijōbu desu ka?**
*kah-doh wah dye-joe-boo dess kah*

---

## 🤔 When You’re Confused or Need Help

**I don’t understand**
**Wakarimasen**
*wah-kah-ree-mah-sen*

**I don’t really understand**
**Chotto wakarimasen**
*cho-ttoh wah-kah-ree-mah-sen*

**Please speak slowly**
**Yukkuri onegaishimasu**
*yoo-koo-ree oh-neh-gai-she-mass*

---

## 🙇‍♂️ Being Extra Polite (High Respect Situations)

**Excuse me (very polite / entering space)**
**Shitsurei shimasu**
*she-tsoo-reh-ee she-mass*

**It’s okay / No problem**
**Daijōbu desu**
*dye-joe-boo dess*

---

## 🚨 Emergencies (Rare but Important)

**Help!**
**Tasukete!**
*tah-soo-keh-teh*

**I’m okay**
**Daijōbu desu**
*dye-joe-boo dess*

---

## ⭐ The 6 Phrases That Carry the Whole Trip

If you remember only these, you’ll be fine:

* **Konnichiwa** — Hello
* **Arigatō gozaimasu** — Thank you very much
* **Sumimasen** — Excuse me / Sorry
* **Onegaishimasu** — Please
* **Kore o onegaishimasu** — This one, please
* **Toire wa doko desu ka?** — Where’s the bathroom?

---

### 📌 Pro Tips

* Speak **slowly**, not loudly
* Bow slightly when thanking
* Effort matters more than correctness
* These phrases unlock patience and kindness
