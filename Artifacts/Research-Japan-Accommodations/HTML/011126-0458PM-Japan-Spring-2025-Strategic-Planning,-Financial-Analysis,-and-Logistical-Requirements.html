  1. Neighborhood Guide (Where to Stay)
    Keyla shared a guide on the "Best Neighborhoods for First-Time Travelers" to help the group decide on a base.
    Neighborhood Vibe Best For...
    1. Shinjuku Iconic, Energetic, Ultra-Connected All-around base. Nonstop transport, nightlife, shopping, and major train connections for day trips.
    2. Shibuya Trendy, Youthful, Central City energy. Famous "Shibuya Crossing," walking distance to Harajuku/Yoyogi Park.
    3. Tokyo Station / Marunouchi Convenient Maximum Access. Best for Shinkansen (bullet train) day trips to Nikko/Hakone. Close to Imperial Palace.
    4. Asakusa Traditional, Historic Old Tokyo Feel. Temples, slower pace, historic sights. Slightly further from central nightlife.
    5. Ginza Upscale, Stylish Luxury & Food. High-end shopping and polished dining. Quieter at night.
