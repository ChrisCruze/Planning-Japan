# Outline

## Logline
A comprehensive trip planning system for Japan that breaks the pattern of last-minute rushed decisions through four interconnected documents (Language, Activities, Logistics, People) that transform trip planning from stress into execution.

## Structure

### Document 1 – Language
- **Goal**: Build confidence through 30-50 functional Japanese phrases that reduce mental freeze in common situations, focusing on recognition and repetition rather than grammar mastery.
- **Key points**:
  - [1.1] Essential phrase categories: Greetings, Navigation, Food, Transactions, Clarification
  - [1.2] Pronunciation guides and contextual usage examples
  - [1.3] Practice recommendations for high-frequency needs (food, navigation, emergencies)
  - [1.4] Cross-reference phrases to [[Activities]] for contextual usage
  - [1.5] Transportation-related phrases linked to [[Logistics]]
- **Depends on facts**: Language Learning (30-50 phrases target), Language Categories

### Document 2 – Activities
- **Goal**: Define the feel and flow of the trip day-to-day, creating clarity and excitement while prioritizing meaningful experiences over maximum quantity.
- **Key points**:
  - [2.1] Environmental context: Spring in April (weather, clothing, walking requirements)
  - [2.2] Core experience categories: Sightseeing, Food & Dining, Cultural Experiences, Shopping & Markets, Nature & Outdoors
  - [2.3] Activity planning principles: Embed rest and spontaneity into flow
  - [2.4] Day-by-day flow concepts (not strict scheduling)
  - [2.5] Link phrases from [[Language]] needed at specific activities
  - [2.6] Location-based transportation needs inform [[Logistics]]
  - [2.7] Group activity preferences from [[People]]
- **Depends on facts**: Trip Dates (April 2-11, 2025), Weather Context, Destination Locations

### Document 3 – Logistics
- **Goal**: Remove uncertainty around travel arrangements by answering "How are we physically getting to, and moving through, Japan?" with an analytical framework for decisions.
- **Key points**:
  - [3.1] Trip schedule: Dates, duration, weather context
  - [3.2] Flight strategy: Origin points (ATL, NYC, Hershey), traveler coordination, booking approach
  - [3.3] Domestic flight considerations (JAL Explorer Pass opportunity)
  - [3.4] Accommodation strategy: Location priorities, hotel/ryokan selection criteria, booking timeline
  - [3.5] Transportation within Japan: JR Pass, local transit passes, inter-city travel
  - [3.6] Budget framework and cost-saving opportunities
  - [3.7] Group coordination needs from [[People]]
  - [3.8] Location-based accommodation needs from [[Activities]]
- **Sub-documents**:
  - [3.9] [[Flights]]: Flight research overview and comparison framework
  - [3.10] Flights/[[Flights-Options-Kayak]]: Kayak flight research and comparison (all origins)
  - [3.11] Flights/[[Flights-Options-Google-Flights]]: Google Flights research and comparison (all origins)
  - [3.12] Hotels/[[Hotels]]: Hotel selection criteria and research framework (Booking.com)
  - [3.13] Hotels/[[Hotel-Options]]: All hotel options being considered

  - [3.15] Packages/[[Package-Options]]: All package options being considered

- **Depends on facts**: Trip Dates, Origin Points, Destination Locations, Transportation, Budget and Pricing, Booking Strategy, Hotel Selection Criteria

## Document Relationships

### Primary Connections
1. **Language ↔ Activities**: Phrases contextualized by activity locations and situations
2. **Activities ↔ Logistics**: Location needs inform transportation and hotel selection
3. **Logistics ↔ People**: Group coordination drives flight and accommodation decisions
4. **People ↔ Activities**: Individual preferences shape activity planning

### Planning Flow
1. **People** document establishes who is traveling and their preferences
2. **Logistics** document addresses how to get there and where to stay
3. **Activities** document defines what to do day-to-day
4. **Language** document builds confidence for real-world interactions

All documents are updated iteratively as planning progresses.

## Document Maintenance

### When to Update Each Document
- **facts.md**: Update first when any canonical detail changes (names, dates, locations, constraints)
- **outline.md**: Update when document structure or relationships change
- **Language**: Update when new phrase categories or contexts are identified
- **Activities**: Update as itinerary details are refined
- **Logistics**: Update when bookings are confirmed or options change
- **People**: Update when traveler preferences or group dynamics change

### Cross-Reference Maintenance
All documents should maintain current cross-references to related documents. When updating one document, check if related documents need updates to maintain consistency.
