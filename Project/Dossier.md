## Flight Research - January 4, 2026

### Question 1: Is it cheaper to fly from Atlanta to Japan or from NYC to Japan?

**Answer: Atlanta is SLIGHTLY cheaper than NYC, but the difference is minimal ($50-150)**

**Atlanta → Tokyo Pricing (April 2026):**
- Delta Direct (ATL→HND): $795-$911 roundtrip
- Budget options with stops: From $318 one-way
- Average range: $800-$1,100 for direct flights
- Flight time: 14 hours 10 minutes direct
- Delta operates 7 direct flights per week (daily service)

**NYC → Tokyo Pricing (April 2026):**
- Direct flights (JFK/EWR→NRT/HND): $800-$1,200 roundtrip
- Budget options with stops: From $244 one-way  
- Average range: $900-$1,200 for direct flights
- Flight time: 14 hours 24 minutes direct
- Multiple carriers: ANA, JAL, United, American

**Key Finding:** The price difference is NOT significant enough to justify flying NYC→ATL→Tokyo just to save money. If you can find a good NYC→Tokyo deal in the $800-1,000 range, fly direct from NYC and meet the family in Tokyo.

- Flies LAX → Tokyo (Narita)
- Bare-bones service but comfortable

**Pricing:**
- LAX → Tokyo: $518 roundtrip (April dates)

**Savings: $200-400**

**Tradeoffs:**
- Longer travel time (need to fly NYC→LAX first)
- Two separate tickets = risk if first flight delays
- Budget carrier = pay extra for everything (bags, meals, seat selection)
- Good reviews for free Wi-Fi, comfortable seats

**When to consider:**
- If budget is PRIMARY concern
- If comfortable with long travel days
- If dates are flexible
