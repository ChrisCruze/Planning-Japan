# Research Japan Flights
## Purpose
Secure optimal Japan flights through comprehensive research comparing airlines, routes, prices, and comfort to book early at best rates with ideal timing and seating for the entire group.

## Vision
Securing perfect Japan flights through thorough research—optimal balance of price, schedule, comfort, and convenience. Booked early for best prices and seat selection.

## Goal
Research 5+ flight options comparing airlines, routes, prices, timing, and comfort. Create comparison spreadsheet analyzing all factors. Present top 2-3 options to cousins. Book flights securing best prices and availability.

## Plan
1. Confirm travel dates and budget with cousins
2. Search multiple platforms, identify 5-7 strong options across price points
3. Deep dive analysis: seat reviews, baggage policies, layover quality, reliability
4. Create comparison spreadsheet with all-in costs
5. Present to cousins, gather feedback, make final decision and book

## Benefits
Early booking ensures availability and better prices, comfortable travel means arriving refreshed, demonstrates leadership to cousins, booking early saves $200-500 per ticket, early seat selection means better seats free.

## Strategy
Start broad, narrow down. Price per hour of comfort (cheapest isn't best value). Flexibility windows (search +/- 3 days). Visual comparison spreadsheet. Book direct when possible. Seat selection immediately.

## Outcome
**Success Criteria:** 5+ flight options researched and documented, Comparison spreadsheet with 10+ data points per option, All options analyzed for total cost, Flights booked at $800-1200 per person, Seats selected for entire group.

**Deliverables:** Flight comparison spreadsheet, top 2-3 options presentation, booked flight confirmations, seat selection confirmations, shared calendar with flight details

## Prompts
Research best airlines for trans-Pacific routes based on recent passenger reviews. Compare typical flight prices by season and booking window to identify optimal timing. Find seat quality reviews for common aircraft types on these routes. Identify which layover airports provide best experience and connection times. Study flight reliability data and on-time performance for major routes to Japan.

## Principles
- Book direct when equal: better customer service
- Total cost matters: include all fees
- Layover sweet spot: avoid under 1 hour or over 4 hours
- Seat selection is worth it: pay for good seats
- Group consensus: get buy-in from everyone

## Questions
- Date flexibility: shift +/- few days for better prices?
- Trip length: 10 days? 14 days?
- Direct flight (14 hrs) vs 1-stop (cheaper)?
- Preferred layover cities if 1-stop?
- Economy vs premium economy vs business?

## Objective
Research 5+ flight options, create comprehensive comparison, present to cousins, and book optimal flights at good prices.

## Brainstorming
- Set Google Flights price alerts for target dates to catch sudden drops
- Check credit card points balance—could offset $200-400 per ticket if booking through Chase/Amex portal
- Search incognito mode to avoid dynamic pricing based on cookies
- Compare booking Tuesday afternoon (when airlines release deals) vs other days

- Research if splitting into two one-ways is cheaper than round-trip

- Create shared Google Doc with flight options where cousins can vote/comment

Secure ideal accommodations across all Japan cities by researching location quality, reviews, and pricing to book early at best rates while balancing modern comfort with authentic traditional experiences.

## Vision
Perfect accommodations in each city balancing location, quality, price, and group comfort. Mix of modern comfort and authentic Japanese experiences. Every booking optimized through research.

## Goal
Research and book accommodations for entire trip. For each city: identify 3-5 options comparing location (transit/attractions proximity), reviews, price, group space, and unique features. Create comparison spreadsheet.

## Plan
1. Finalize cities, nights per city, accommodation style preferences with cousins
2. Research 3-5 options per city: location analysis, reviews, price comparison
3. Create accommodation spreadsheet comparing all options
4. Check availability for exact dates, calculate total trip cost
5. Present options to cousins, gather input, book all accommodations

## Benefits
Strategic locations save hours of travel time, quality accommodations mean better rest, mix of styles creates memorable variety, proximity to transit simplifies navigation, early booking gets better rates.

## Strategy
Location trumps luxury (near transit beats fancy hotel far away). Mix of styles (ryokan + modern hotels). Read recent reviews religiously. Free cancellation when possible. Check transit proximity (10-min walk to station).

## Outcome
**Success Criteria:** All accommodations researched (3-5 options per city), Comparison spreadsheet complete, All nights booked with confirmations, Average $100-200 per night achieved, At least one unique experience (ryokan), All locations within 10-min walk of transit.

🗾 The Four-Document System for Planning a Japan Trip

A Strategic Framework for Travel Confidence, Clarity, and Connection

“Each document answers a different question — together, they create the full experience.”

- Shifts focus from perfection to confidence and clarity[^2]

🗣️ Document One: Language & Learning Japanese

🎯 Purpose: Build Confidence, Not Fluency

This document exists for one reason: confidence — not grammar mastery.

The aim is to internalize ~30–50 functional Japanese phrases that:

- Reduce mental freeze in common situations
- Focus on recognition and repetition
- Target high-frequency needs like food, navigation, and emergencies

#### 🗨️ Language Categories

| Category        | Focused Keywords & Phrases                           |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| Greetings   | Hello, thank you, excuse me, sorry                    |
| Navigation  | Station, train, exit, entrance, bathroom, platform   |
| Food        | Menu, recommendation, no meat, not spicy, delicious  |
| Transactions| How much?, cash or card?, receipt, OK                |
| Clarification| I don’t understand, please help, repeat that        |

## Goal
Learn 30-50 essential Japanese phrases covering greetings, courtesy, food ordering, directions, shopping, emergencies. Create reference sheet/app with phrases by context. Achieve basic pronunciation. Understand hiragana/katakana for station names.

🇯🇵 Japan Survival Japanese — One-Page Cheat Sheet

(Pronunciation is approximate; speak slowly and confidently)

---

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Greeting & Politeness (Use Everywhere)

Hello / Good afternoon
Konnichiwa
kon-nee-chee-wah

Thank you very much
Arigatō gozaimasu
ah-ree-gah-toe go-zai-mass

Excuse me / Sorry / Thank you for the trouble
Sumimasen
soo-mee-mah-sen

Please / I humbly request
Onegaishimasu
oh-neh-gai-she-mass

---

🍽️ Entering & Eating at Restaurants

This one, please (pointing)
Kore o onegaishimasu
ko-reh oh oh-neh-gai-she-mass

Water, please
Mizu o kudasai
mee-zoo oh koo-dah-sai

Beer, please
Bīru o kudasai
bee-roo oh koo-dah-sai

Is this okay? / Is this fine?
Daijōbu desu ka?
dye-joe-boo dess kah

Thank you for the meal (after eating)
Gochisōsama deshita
go-chee-so-sah-mah desh-tah

---

🚆 Getting Around & Transportation

Where is the station?
Eki wa doko desu ka?
eh-kee wah doh-koh dess kah

Where is the train?
Densha wa doko desu ka?
den-shah wah doh-koh dess kah

Where is the bathroom?
Toire wa doko desu ka?
toy-reh wah doh-koh dess kah

Which line?
Nan-sen desu ka?
nahn-sen dess kah

---

🛍️ Shopping & Paying

How much is this?
Ikura desu ka?
ee-koo-rah dess kah

I’ll take this
Kore o kudasai
ko-reh oh koo-dah-sai

Credit card OK?
Kādo wa daijōbu desu ka?
kah-doh wah dye-joe-boo dess kah

🤔 When You’re Confused or Need Help

I don’t understand
Wakarimasen
wah-kah-ree-mah-sen

I don’t really understand

cho-ttoh wah-kah-ree-mah-sen

Yukkuri onegaishimasu
yoo-koo-ree oh-neh-gai-she-mass

It’s okay / No problem

dye-joe-boo dess

🚨 Emergencies (Rare but Important)

I’m okay

dye-joe-boo dess
