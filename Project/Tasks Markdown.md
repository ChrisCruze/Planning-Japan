# Research Japan Flights
## Purpose
Secure optimal Japan flights through comprehensive research comparing airlines, routes, prices, and comfort to book early at best rates with ideal timing and seating for the entire group.

## Vision
Securing perfect Japan flights through thorough research—optimal balance of price, schedule, comfort, and convenience. Booked early for best prices and seat selection.

## Goal
Research 5+ flight options comparing airlines, routes, prices, timing, and comfort. Create comparison spreadsheet analyzing all factors. Present top 2-3 options to cousins. Book flights securing best prices and availability.

## Plan
1. Confirm travel dates and budget with cousins
2. Search multiple platforms, identify 5-7 strong options across price points
3. Deep dive analysis: seat reviews, baggage policies, layover quality, reliability
4. Create comparison spreadsheet with all-in costs
5. Present to cousins, gather feedback, make final decision and book

## Benefits
Early booking ensures availability and better prices, comfortable travel means arriving refreshed, demonstrates leadership to cousins, booking early saves $200-500 per ticket, early seat selection means better seats free.

## Strategy
Start broad, narrow down. Price per hour of comfort (cheapest isn't best value). Flexibility windows (search +/- 3 days). Visual comparison spreadsheet. Book direct when possible. Seat selection immediately.

## Outcome
**Success Criteria:** 5+ flight options researched and documented, Comparison spreadsheet with 10+ data points per option, All options analyzed for total cost, Flights booked at $800-1200 per person, Seats selected for entire group.

**Deliverables:** Flight comparison spreadsheet, top 2-3 options presentation, booked flight confirmations, seat selection confirmations, shared calendar with flight details

## Prompts
Research best airlines for trans-Pacific routes based on recent passenger reviews. Compare typical flight prices by season and booking window to identify optimal timing. Find seat quality reviews for common aircraft types on these routes. Identify which layover airports provide best experience and connection times. Study flight reliability data and on-time performance for major routes to Japan.

## Principles
- Book direct when equal: better customer service
- Total cost matters: include all fees
- Layover sweet spot: avoid under 1 hour or over 4 hours
- Seat selection is worth it: pay for good seats
- Group consensus: get buy-in from everyone

## Questions
- Date flexibility: shift +/- few days for better prices?
- Trip length: 10 days? 14 days?
- Direct flight (14 hrs) vs 1-stop (cheaper)?
- Preferred layover cities if 1-stop?
- Economy vs premium economy vs business?

## Objective
Research 5+ flight options, create comprehensive comparison, present to cousins, and book optimal flights at good prices.

## Brainstorming
- Set Google Flights price alerts for target dates to catch sudden drops
- Check credit card points balance—could offset $200-400 per ticket if booking through Chase/Amex portal
- Search incognito mode to avoid dynamic pricing based on cookies
- Compare booking Tuesday afternoon (when airlines release deals) vs other days
- Check airline alliance partnerships for potential upgrades or lounge access
- Research if splitting into two one-ways is cheaper than round-trip
- Use ExpertFlyer to check real-time seat availability before booking
- Create shared Google Doc with flight options where cousins can vote/comment
- Book refundable fare initially, monitor prices for 24 hours, cancel if better deal appears
- Check if any cousins have airline status that could benefit group (priority boarding, free bags)

# Research Japan Accommodations
## Purpose
Secure ideal accommodations across all Japan cities by researching location quality, reviews, and pricing to book early at best rates while balancing modern comfort with authentic traditional experiences.

## Vision
Perfect accommodations in each city balancing location, quality, price, and group comfort. Mix of modern comfort and authentic Japanese experiences. Every booking optimized through research.

## Goal
Research and book accommodations for entire trip. For each city: identify 3-5 options comparing location (transit/attractions proximity), reviews, price, group space, and unique features. Create comparison spreadsheet.

## Plan
1. Finalize cities, nights per city, accommodation style preferences with cousins
2. Research 3-5 options per city: location analysis, reviews, price comparison
3. Create accommodation spreadsheet comparing all options
4. Check availability for exact dates, calculate total trip cost
5. Present options to cousins, gather input, book all accommodations

## Benefits
Strategic locations save hours of travel time, quality accommodations mean better rest, mix of styles creates memorable variety, proximity to transit simplifies navigation, early booking gets better rates.

## Strategy
Location trumps luxury (near transit beats fancy hotel far away). Mix of styles (ryokan + modern hotels). Read recent reviews religiously. Free cancellation when possible. Check transit proximity (10-min walk to station).

## Outcome
**Success Criteria:** All accommodations researched (3-5 options per city), Comparison spreadsheet complete, All nights booked with confirmations, Average $100-200 per night achieved, At least one unique experience (ryokan), All locations within 10-min walk of transit.

**Deliverables:** Accommodation comparison spreadsheet, booking confirmations for all nights, shared trip document with addresses/check-in info, neighborhood guides for each location

## Prompts
Research best tourist neighborhoods in Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka based on transit access and walkability. Find recent traveler reviews comparing hotel quality and location value. Identify traditional ryokan options that are foreigner-friendly with reasonable prices. Study typical accommodation pricing by season to gauge fair rates. Learn which areas to avoid despite appearing central on maps.

## Principles
- Location trumps luxury: excellent location in simple hotel beats fancy hotel far away
- Recent reviews matter most: last 3-6 months only
- Calculate true cost: include all fees
- One splurge (ryokan), rest practical
- Neighborhood over property

## Questions
- Cities visiting: Tokyo/Kyoto/Osaka or others?
- Nights per city allocation?
- Accommodation style: Western hotels, Airbnb, ryokan, mix?
- Separate rooms or share to save money?
- Budget per night: $100? $150? $200?

## Objective
Research and book quality accommodations for entire trip ensuring excellent transit-proximate locations, good value, and mix of comfort and unique experiences.

## Brainstorming
- Use Google Maps satellite view to verify actual walking distance to station (not just listed distance)
- Check Booking.com "Genius" deals or Amex Fine Hotels & Resorts for potential upgrades
- Search Japan-specific booking sites (Rakuten Travel, Jalan) for better ryokan rates
- Join hotel Facebook groups to ask recent travelers about specific properties
- Book properties with 24-hour reception to avoid issues with late flight arrivals
- Use Street View to walk the route from station to hotel virtually, check for hills/stairs
- Reserve one centrally-located property, then book day trips from there vs moving hotels constantly
- Check if any properties offer luggage forwarding service between cities (common in Japan)
- Look for hotels with coin laundry to pack lighter and wash clothes mid-trip
- Create backup list of 2-3 alternatives per city in case primary choices sell out

# Learn Essential Japanese
## Purpose
Acquire functional Japanese language skills through systematic learning of essential phrases, proper pronunciation, and basic character recognition to navigate daily situations and show cultural respect during the trip.

## Vision
Arriving in Japan with functional language skills to greet politely, order food confidently, ask directions, handle emergencies. Not fluency, but enough to navigate situations and show respect through language.

## Goal
Learn 30-50 essential Japanese phrases covering greetings, courtesy, food ordering, directions, shopping, emergencies. Create reference sheet/app with phrases by context. Achieve basic pronunciation. Understand hiragana/katakana for station names.

## Plan
1. Download Duolingo, complete initial lessons, choose learning resources
2. Learn essential phrases by category: greetings, food, directions, shopping, emergencies
3. Practice daily: Duolingo + speaking aloud, learn hiragana and katakana basics
4. Create physical reference card and phone note with all phrases
5. Final intensive practice before departure

## Benefits
Order food confidently, navigate stations, show respect to locals, unlock authentic interactions, read basic signs and station names, reduce stress when English unavailable.

## Strategy
Daily consistency over marathons (15 min/day). Focus on survival phrases not grammar. Pronunciation matters—invest in getting sounds right. Audio learning for commutes. Physical reference card as backup. Practice aloud.

## Outcome
**Success Criteria:** 30-50 essential phrases learned and recallable, Proper pronunciation practiced, Navigate common scenarios: ordering/directions/courtesy, Hiragana/katakana learned for station recognition, Physical reference card created, Phone has offline phrase list.

**Deliverables:** Master phrase list (30-50 phrases), laminated reference card, phone note with phrases, hiragana/katakana chart, pronunciation guide, Duolingo streak

## Prompts
Research most essential Japanese survival phrases for tourists based on recent traveler experiences. Find pronunciation guides for challenging Japanese sounds that English speakers struggle with. Identify best free and paid resources for learning practical conversational Japanese quickly. Study hiragana and katakana learning methods focused on reading recognition. Discover common mistakes foreigners make when attempting Japanese phrases.

## Principles
- Effort appreciated over perfection: attempt is valued
- Pronunciation before vocabulary: 10 phrases correctly beats 50 unintelligibly
- Context matters: understand when to use phrases
- Politeness built-in: learn polite forms (desu/masu)
- Daily practice non-negotiable: 15 min minimum

## Questions
- Duolingo sufficient or supplement with Pimsleur?
- 30-50 phrases realistic or focus on 20 most critical?
- How important is hiragana/katakana vs just phrases?
- Should I learn numbers or rely on written numbers?
- Group learning with cousins or solo?

## Objective
Learn 30-50 essential phrases with proper pronunciation, understand basic hiragana/katakana for reading signs, create reference materials, practice consistently to enable basic communication and show cultural respect.

## Brainstorming
- Create Anki flashcard deck with audio pronunciation from native speakers

- Record myself saying phrases, send to Japanese friend/tutor for pronunciation feedback

🇯🇵 Japan Survival Japanese — One-Page Cheat Sheet

(Pronunciation is approximate; speak slowly and confidently)

---

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Greeting & Politeness (Use Everywhere)

Hello / Good afternoon
Konnichiwa
kon-nee-chee-wah

Thank you very much
Arigatō gozaimasu
ah-ree-gah-toe go-zai-mass

Excuse me / Sorry / Thank you for the trouble
Sumimasen
soo-mee-mah-sen

Please / I humbly request
Onegaishimasu
oh-neh-gai-she-mass

---

🍽️ Entering & Eating at Restaurants

This one, please (pointing)
Kore o onegaishimasu
ko-reh oh oh-neh-gai-she-mass

Water, please
Mizu o kudasai
mee-zoo oh koo-dah-sai

Beer, please
Bīru o kudasai
bee-roo oh koo-dah-sai

Is this okay? / Is this fine?
Daijōbu desu ka?
dye-joe-boo dess kah

Thank you for the meal (after eating)
Gochisōsama deshita
go-chee-so-sah-mah desh-tah

---

🚆 Getting Around & Transportation

Where is the station?
Eki wa doko desu ka?
eh-kee wah doh-koh dess kah

Where is the train?
Densha wa doko desu ka?
den-shah wah doh-koh dess kah

Where is the bathroom?
Toire wa doko desu ka?
toy-reh wah doh-koh dess kah

Which line?
Nan-sen desu ka?
nahn-sen dess kah

---

🛍️ Shopping & Paying

How much is this?

I’ll take this

ko-reh oh koo-dah-sai

Credit card OK?
Kādo wa daijōbu desu ka?
kah-doh wah dye-joe-boo dess kah

🤔 When You’re Confused or Need Help
