This project is complete when:

1. Flight Tickets Booked: Flights selected and purchased for best price/timing/comfort. Booked at least 2-3 months before departure. Includes documented comparison of at least 5 flight options with cost/timing analysis.

2. Accommodations Secured: Hotels/Airbnbs booked for each location based on: location quality (proximity to activities/transit), reviews and ratings, price optimization, group comfort (space for everyone). Documented comparison of options.

3. Comprehensive Itinerary Created: Day-by-day plan including must-see attractions with timing/admission, restaurant recommendations with ratings/reservation status, transportation between locations, free time and flexibility, backup options for bad weather. Shared with cousins and refined based on input.

4. Language Preparation: Learned and practiced at least 30-50 essential Japanese phrases (greetings/courtesy, ordering food, asking directions, emergency phrases). Created reference sheet or app.

5. Research Synthesis: Compiled detailed research on best neighborhoods per city, cultural etiquette and customs, transportation systems (JR Pass, metro cards), food specialties by region, budget estimates for daily expenses. All documented in shareable format.

6. Presentation to Cousins: Presented full plan in engaging format (document/slide deck/collaborative tool) and received positive feedback.
