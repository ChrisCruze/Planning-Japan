For a trip visiting Tokyo, Kyoto, and Osaka, this transforms a potentially expensive multi-city itinerary into something financially comparable to staying in one city.

This discovery answers a question I didn't even know I had: "How do I afford visiting multiple cities without the trip becoming prohibitively expensive?" The answer isn't the JR Rail Pass (which costs $280-380 for 7 days) - it's strategically choosing JAL for the international flight specifically to unlock free domestic travel.

It's a legitimate option if budget is paramount, but not compelling enough to be the default choice.
