## Planning Constraints

### Group Size Considerations
- **5+ travelers**: Separate bookings recommended (no group leverage in packages)
- **Room Requirements**: 2-3 rooms needed for group
- **Booking Approach**: Individual preferences vs. group coordination balance

### Booking Strategy
- **Flights**: Book directly with airline (better IRROPS handling)
- **Hotels**: Separate booking recommended for groups of 5+
- **Packages**: Not recommended (DIY bookings consistently match or beat prices)

### Hotel Selection Criteria (Priority Order)
1. **Location** > Room size
2. Close to public transit (<5 minute walk)
3. In lively yet accessible neighborhoods
4. Walking distance to key areas

## Key Decisions and Frameworks

### Flight Decision Framework
- JAL if prices competitive → Unlock free domestic flights
- Direct NYC → Tokyo if finding $800-1,000 deals → Meet family in Tokyo
- ZIPAIR budget option if $700-800 → Sacrifice time for savings
- Join ATL family flight if $950-1,200 → Shared experience worth premium

### Hotel Selection Framework
- **Questions to Answer**:
  - How close to public transit? (Target: <5 minute walk)
  - What neighborhood vibe? (See Activities document)
  - What's the group size? (2-3 rooms for 5 travelers)
  - What's the budget range? (~$2,500 per person including flights)

### Arrival Logistics
- **Recommendation**: Pre-book airport transfer for arrival (Airport Limousine Bus or private van)
- **Rationale**: Navigating Tokyo trains with 5 people + luggage during peak times is high-stress
- **Daily Travel**: Switch to public transit (Suica/Pasmo) once luggage is dropped

## Success Metrics

The planning process is successful when:
1. Flights booked 2-3 months in advance
2. Accommodations reserved with confirmation numbers
3. Itinerary complete with detailed daily schedule and backup options
4. Language proficiency: 30-50 phrases memorized and practiced
5. Research documentation compiled (transportation, customs, emergency contacts, local tips)

## Planning Phases

1. **Research** (Weeks 1-2): Destination research, route planning, date selection
2. **Booking** (Weeks 3-4): Flight booking, accommodation reservations
3. **Itinerary** (Weeks 5-8): Detailed daily planning, restaurant reservations
4. **Language** (Weeks 5-12): Daily phrase practice, pronunciation training
5. **Preparation** (Weeks 9-12): Final confirmations, packing lists, document compilation
