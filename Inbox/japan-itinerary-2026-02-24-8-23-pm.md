To replicate this success in your own planning, borrow these two mental models:

**The Anchor and Float Framework**

Everything else—lunch, neighborhood wandering, shopping—is the "Float." The Float fills the space organically around the Anchor without the pressure of a strict timeline.

**The "Weakest Link" Energy Budget**
In a group of six (including five adults and one youth), the group can only move as fast as its most tired member. You must budget your daily steps and activities around the person with the lowest stamina, not the highest. Building in dedicated "sit down" times ensures the group stays intact and happy.
