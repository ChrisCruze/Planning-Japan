# Day 2 — Saturday, April 4: See the Whole City Before You Explore It

## Hop-on hop-off bus to get the lay of the land, Shibuya Crossing for the iconic moment, then let the group scatter based on what catches their eye.

- **Morning: Hop-on Hop-off Bus.** The Sky Hop Bus is the best way to understand Tokyo’s geography on day one. All-day pass lets you board and exit freely at major stops. Budget approximately $32/pp. Book at [skyhopbus.com](https://skyhopbus.com/purchases/new) or buy at the bus stop.
- **Late morning: Shibuya Crossing.** The world’s busiest pedestrian intersection. Cross it, then watch it from above — the Starbucks overlooking the crossing or Mag’s Park rooftop both give the best vantage point. This is a 30-minute stop that everyone will remember.
- **Afternoon: Sightseeing, walking, shopping, eating.** Use the bus loop as your guide. Get off wherever looks interesting. Reference Chris’s activity list for options. Asakusa, Harajuku, Shinjuku — the bus connects them all.
- **Evening: First real dinner in Tokyo.** This is the night to find an izakaya (Japanese pub) and try a few things — yakitori, edamame, beer. Look for places with plastic food displays outside the door and a lively crowd inside.
- **Cherry blossom spotting starts today.** Ueno Park, Chidorigafuchi, and Meguro River are the top sakura viewing spots in Tokyo. If the bus passes near any of them, get off and take a walk. Early April is peak bloom.
