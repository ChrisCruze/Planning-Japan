### 🏪 Convenience Store (Konbini) Culture — Don’t Skip

- **[7-Eleven Japan](https://www.sej.co.jp/services/en.html)**, **FamilyMart**, **Lawson** — 24 hours, on every block
- Onigiri (rice balls): ¥130 (~$0.84) each — fresh and filling
- Cheap daily breakfast: ¥300–500 (~$1.95–$3.25) per person

### 🍱 Day Trip Food

**Kamakura:** Bring cash. Try **shirasu** (whitebait) rice bowls — a local specialty. Komachi Street has matcha soft serve.
**Mt. Fuji:** Pack konbini snacks the night before. 5th Station has a food hall with curry and noodles.

## Packing & Practical Tips

### What to Pack for April

- **Layers** — mornings cool (10–12°C), afternoons pleasant (15–20°C)
- **Jacket** — mandatory for Mt. Fuji; significantly colder at elevation
- **Comfortable sneakers** — Keyla’s explicit requirement for Kamakura + Fuji; expect 15,000–25,000 steps daily
- Small day backpack for each person
- Portable umbrella — light rain possible
- **Passport** — required on Mt. Fuji day (per spreadsheet notes)
- **Credit card** — bring on Mt. Fuji day (per spreadsheet notes)

### Japan Etiquette Essentials

- **Remove shoes** at traditional restaurants, ryokans, and any raised-floor entry
- **No eating while walking** on public streets — find a spot
- **Trash cans are rare** — carry your trash until you find a bin (konbini have them)
- **Cash is king** — carry ¥10,000–20,000 (~$64–$129) at all times; many places are cash-only
- **Be quiet on trains** — no phone calls, keep voices low
- **Always queue** — Japan takes lines very seriously; never cut
- **Bow when greeted** — a small nod works fine for tourists

### Money & Payments

- **ATMs:** Use [7-Eleven ATMs](https://www.sej.co.jp/services/en.html) — reliably accept foreign cards, English menus
- **[Suica/Pasmo](https://www.jreast.co.jp/e/pass/suica.html):** Load ¥10,000 (~$64.50) at arrival; swipe for everything
- **Exchange rate:** **$1 = ¥155** (as of Feb 20, 2026) — favorable for Americans · [Check live rate](https://www.xe.com/en-us/currencyconverter/convert/?Amount=1&From=USD&To=JPY)
- **Tax-free shopping:** Show passport at department stores for purchases over ¥5,000 (~$32.25) — saves 10%
