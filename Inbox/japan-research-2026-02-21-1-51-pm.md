After burning all those calories on the mountain, the video pivots to "Kyoto's Kitchen"—the legendary Nishiki Market. This five-block-long, narrow shopping street is an absolute sensory overload in the best way possible. It's packed with over a hundred stalls selling everything from fresh seafood and pickled vegetables to high-end Japanese knives.

The creator gives a crucial piece of advice here: do not walk and eat. While it’s tempting to grab a skewer and keep strolling, it's considered quite rude in Japan.
