# Language & Learning Japanese

> Build confidence, not fluency. Internalize 30-50 functional Japanese phrases that reduce mental freeze in common situations.

## Key Takeaways

- **Purpose**: Build confidence through functional Japanese phrases (30-50 phrases)
- **Focus**: Recognition and repetition, not grammar mastery
- **Target**: High-frequency needs like food, navigation, and emergencies
- **Approach**: Even broken Japanese delivered confidently softens interactions

## Purpose: Build Confidence, Not Fluency

This document exists for one reason: **confidence** — not grammar mastery.

The aim is to internalize ~30–50 **functional Japanese phrases** that:

- Reduce **mental freeze** in common situations
- Focus on **recognition and repetition**
- Target **high-frequency needs** like food, navigation, and emergencies

> [!tip]
> Even broken Japanese delivered confidently softens interactions.

## Language Categories

| Category        | Focused Keywords & Phrases                           |
|----------------|-------------------------------------------------------|
| **Greetings**   | Hello, thank you, excuse me, sorry                    |
| **Navigation**  | Station, train, exit, entrance, bathroom, platform   |
| **Food**        | Menu, recommendation, no meat, not spicy, delicious  |
| **Transactions**| How much?, cash or card?, receipt, OK                |
| **Clarification**| I don't understand, please help, repeat that        |

### Greetings

Essential phrases for daily interactions:
- Hello: Konnichiwa (こんにちは)
- Thank you: Arigatou gozaimasu (ありがとうございます)
- Excuse me: Sumimasen (すみません)
- Sorry: Gomen nasai (ごめんなさい)

### Navigation

Critical phrases for getting around:
- Station: Eki (駅)
- Train: Densha (電車)
- Exit: Deguchi (出口)
- Entrance: Iriguchi (入口)
- Bathroom: Toire (トイレ)
- Platform: Hōmu (ホーム)

### Food

Essential phrases for dining:
- Menu: Menyū (メニュー)
- Recommendation: Osusume (おすすめ)
- No meat: Nikunashi (肉なし)
- Not spicy: Karakunai (辛くない)
- Delicious: Oishii (美味しい)

### Transactions

Key phrases for purchases:
- How much?: Ikura desu ka? (いくらですか？)
- Cash or card?: Genkin ka kādo ka? (現金かカードか？)
- Receipt: Ryōshūsho (領収書)
- OK: Daijōbu (大丈夫)

### Clarification

Important phrases when you need help:
- I don't understand: Wakarimasen (わかりません)
- Please help: Tasukete kudasai (助けてください)
- Repeat that: Mou ichido (もう一度)

### Daily Practice Routine

1. **Morning Review** (5 minutes)
   - Review 5-10 phrases from one category
   - Practice pronunciation out loud

2. **Listening Practice** (10 minutes)
   - Use language learning apps
   - Watch Japanese content with subtitles

3. **Evening Reinforcement** (5 minutes)
   - Review phrases used during the day
   - Add new phrases as needed
