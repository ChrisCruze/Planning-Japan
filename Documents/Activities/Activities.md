> *"What does this trip feel like day-to-day?"*

## Environmental Context: Spring in April

- **Mild temperatures** — comfortable for extended walking
- **Layered clothing recommended** — prepare for variable weather
- **Comfortable for walking** — essential in urban Japan
- **Beautiful cherry blossoms** — peak viewing season in many areas

> [!note]
> Walking is essential in urban Japan. Plan activities with comfortable footwear and manageable distances.

## Core Experience Categories

### 1. Sightseeing

Shrines, temples, neighborhoods, iconic sites

**Examples:**
- Historic temples and shrines
- Traditional neighborhoods (Gion, Asakusa)
- Iconic landmarks (Tokyo Tower, Fushimi Inari)
- Scenic viewpoints and gardens

### 2. Food Exploration

Ramen, street food, conbini meals, izakaya

**Examples:**
- Ramen shops (various regional styles)
- Street food markets (Tsukiji, Nishiki)
- Convenience store (conbini) meals
- Izakaya (Japanese pub) experiences
- Traditional kaiseki dining

### 3. Cultural Moments

Markets, gardens, museums, quiet streets

**Examples:**
- Traditional markets
- Japanese gardens
- Museums and cultural centers
- Quiet neighborhood walks
- Traditional crafts and workshops

### 4. Flex Time

Unstructured hours to wander, rest, or discover

**Purpose:**
- Allow for spontaneity
- Rest and recovery
- Local discoveries
- Group flexibility

## Energy Management

### Core Principles

- Prioritize *meaningful* activities over maximum quantity
- Embed rest and spontaneity into the flow
- Use this doc to answer:
  - "Is this trip relaxed or packed?"
  - "What should I pack?"
  - "What are we *actually* doing?"

### Daily Rhythm

| Time of Day | Energy Level | Activity Type |
|-------------|--------------|---------------|
| **Morning** | High | Sightseeing, active exploration |
| **Midday** | Medium | Food experiences, markets |
| **Afternoon** | Variable | Cultural moments, flexible time |
| **Evening** | Lower | Dining, relaxed activities |

### Balance Guidelines

- **Structured mornings** — high-energy activities when fresh
- **Flexible afternoons** — allow for rest or spontaneous discovery
- **Relaxed evenings** — focus on food and connection
- **Rest days** — build in recovery time
