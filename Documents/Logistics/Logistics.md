### Flight Options

#### Option 1: Meet in Tokyo
- Each group books separately from their origin
- Meet at Tokyo airport or hotel
- **Pros**: Flexibility, individual preferences
- **Cons**: Less shared travel experience

#### Option 2: Fly Together
- Coordinate departure from single origin (likely NYC or ATL)
- **Pros**: Shared experience, easier coordination
- **Cons**: May require additional domestic flights for some travelers

### Route Considerations

| Factor | Options | Notes |
|--------|---------|-------|
| **Direct vs. One-Stop** | Direct preferred for convenience | One-stop may offer cost savings |
| **Arrival Airport** | Narita (NRT) or Haneda (HND) | Haneda closer to Tokyo, Narita often cheaper |
| **Airline Comparison** | JAL, ANA, Delta, United, ZIPAIR | Compare prices, baggage, reputation |

### Key Discovery: JAL Free Domestic Flights

> [!tip]
> **JAL Explorer Pass**: Book JAL international ticket → Get UNLIMITED free domestic flights in Japan (only pay ~$100 in taxes). Saves $500-700+ vs buying domestic flights separately.

**Decision Framework:**
- JAL if prices competitive → Unlock free domestic flights
- Direct NYC → Tokyo if finding $800-1,000 deals → Meet family in Tokyo
- ZIPAIR budget option if $700-800 → Sacrifice time for savings
- Join ATL family flight if $950-1,200 → Shared experience worth premium

## Packages vs. Separate Booking

| Booking Style | Pros | Cons |
|---------------|------|------|
| **Package Deal** | Simplicity, upfront savings, coordinated timing | Less flexibility, limited hotel selection |
| **Separate Booking** | More control, better hotel matching, individual preferences | Slightly more planning effort, may cost slightly more |

### Recommendation

**Separate booking** recommended for:
- Better hotel location matching (see [[Activities]] for location needs)
- Individual traveler preferences (see [[People]] for group dynamics)
- Flexibility to adjust if plans change

## Hotel Criteria

### Priority Order

1. **Location** > Room size
   - Close to **public transit** (train/subway stations)
   - In lively yet accessible **neighborhoods**
   - Walking distance to key areas

2. **Transit Access**
   - Near major train/subway lines
   - Easy access to airport connections
   - Convenient for daily activities

3. **Neighborhood Character**
   - Safe and walkable
   - Local restaurants and amenities nearby
   - Balance of convenience and local experience

> [!note]
> Low friction = high enjoyment. Prioritize location over luxury.

## Transportation Within Japan

### JR Pass Consideration

- **7-day pass**: ~$300-350
- **14-day pass**: ~$500-550
- **Value calculation**: Compare pass cost vs. individual ticket costs
- **Alternative**: Regional passes or individual tickets may be cheaper for shorter stays

### Local Transportation

- **IC Cards**: Suica, Pasmo for local trains/buses
- **City passes**: Tokyo Metro passes, Kyoto bus passes
- **Walking**: Primary mode in urban areas
