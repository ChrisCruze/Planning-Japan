## Recommendation

**Book the Air Premia flight from JFK on April 3, 2026, returning April 11, 2026, at $556.**

This itinerary offers the lowest price across all six origin airports while meeting all reasonable constraints (single stop, ≤26 hour duration, good date alignment). The $24-164 savings compared to other NYC metro airports (EWR, LGA) and $131-164 savings compared to central Pennsylvania airports (MDT, PHL, BWI) justify the trip to JFK for most travelers.

**Airports to Ignore**: 
- **MDT** ($687, 2 stops outbound, longest duration) — dominated by all other options
- **BWI** ($720, longest duration among single-stop options) — most expensive with no advantages
- **LGA** ($625) — if EWR is accessible, save $45 with similar routing

**Edge Cases Excluded**: 
- Ultra-low-cost options with 2+ stops and 25+ hour durations were intentionally excluded per constraints
- Itineraries with overnight 15-20 hour layovers were excluded unless >$300 cheaper (none found)
- Basic economy fares with restrictive baggage policies were excluded
